## Self-Paced Course

This repo hosts a self-paced, gated course system alongside the PhD research work.

- `course/PROGRESS.md` — tracks progress through the course
- `course/lessons/` — lesson materials
- `course/exams/` — exams for each gate
- `course/exams/.keys/` — hidden marking keys — do not open
- `course/answers/` — submitted answers
- `course/grades/` — recorded grades
- `sources/` — curriculum, articles, books

Agents: `tutor`, `examiner`, `grader` (see `.claude/agents/`).
